### 1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy explains what ShopSphere collects, why, how it is shared, and customer choices.
1.2 It applies to website, mobile app, customer support calls, and SMS or email communications.


### 2. Information Collected - Account
2.1 Name, mobile number, email address, password in encrypted form, delivery addresses, and language preference.
2.2 Support communications, tickets, call recordings after notice, and feedback.


### 3. Information Collected - Orders and Payments
3.1 Order ID, items, amounts, shipping and billing addresses, tracking status, return and refund records.
3.2 Payment method type, transaction ID, and status. ShopSphere does not store full card numbers, CVV, OTPs, or net-banking passwords. These are processed by licensed payment providers.
3.3 For COD refunds: bank account number, IFSC, and UPI ID provided for refund only.


### 4. Device and Technical Information
4.1 Device model, operating system, app version, IP address, login timestamps, and error logs.
4.2 Approximate location derived from PIN code or IP for delivery estimation and fraud prevention. Precise GPS is collected only with app permission and can be withdrawn.


### 5. Cookies
5.1 Essential cookies for login, cart, and checkout. Functional cookies for language and preferences. Analytics cookies for aggregated usage.
5.2 Customer can disable non-essential cookies in browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent checkout.


### 6. How Information Is Used - Fulfillment
6.1 To process, pack, dispatch, deliver, track, and handle returns, replacements, and refunds.
6.2 To send order confirmations, dispatch alerts, delivery attempts, and refund status.


### 7. Customer Support Use
7.1 To verify identity by order ID and registered contact, diagnose issues, and maintain ticket history.
7.2 To record refund reference IDs and escalation outcomes.


### 8. Fraud and Security Use
8.1 To detect duplicate payments, false damage claims, account takeover, and return abuse.
8.2 To suspend COD or restrict accounts after notice for verified abuse.


### 9. Analytics
9.1 Aggregated, de-identified data is used to improve search, delivery timelines, and support quality.
9.2 Analytics never includes card details or refund bank account numbers.


### 10. Data Sharing
10.1 **Shared only for:** delivery with carriers (name, address, phone), payments with gateways (transaction data), warranty with manufacturers for covered claims (model, serial, invoice), and legal compliance when required by lawful order.
10.2 **Never sold:** ShopSphere never sells personal data for marketing.


### 11. Service Providers
11.1 Providers include cloud hosting, SMS and email vendors, payment gateways, and logistics partners.
11.2 Providers access data only on documented instructions and are required to protect it and delete it after purpose completion.


### 12. Data Retention
12.1 Active account data is retained while the account is active.
12.2 Order, invoice, and refund records are retained for 5 years from transaction date for accounting and dispute resolution, then archived or deleted.
12.3 Support tickets and call records are retained for 2 years.
12.4 Refund bank details are retained for 1 year after refund completion, then deleted.


### 13. Data Security
13.1 Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, OTP verification, and audit logging.
13.2 In case of a security incident affecting customer data, ShopSphere notifies affected customers by email within 72 hours of confirmation with protective steps.


### 14. Customer Rights
14.1 Customer may: access order and account data, correct name, address, and contact details, request data copy, and withdraw marketing consent at any time.
14.2 Requests are fulfilled within 15 business days after identity verification.


### 15. Account and Data Deletion
15.1 Customer may request account deletion by email from registered email with reason.
15.2 **Eligible for immediate deletion:** profile, addresses, preferences, and marketing data.
15.3 **Conditional:** account with pending delivery, return, or refund is deleted only after settlement, within 30 calendar days of final settlement.
15.4 Order invoices required for legal retention are retained in anonymized form and not used for marketing.


### 16. Marketing Communications
16.1 Promotional SMS, email, and app notifications are sent only with consent. Every message includes opt-out.
16.2 Transactional messages for orders, delivery, and refunds are not promotional and cannot be disabled while an order is active.


### 17. Children's Data
17.1 ShopSphere does not knowingly offer accounts to persons under 18. Accounts detected as underage are suspended pending guardian verification.


### 18. Data Transfers
18.1 Data is stored in India. If processing requires transfer outside India for hosting or support tools, equivalent protection is applied by contract and customer is notified in this Policy update.


### 19. Policy Updates
19.1 Material changes are notified 15 calendar days in advance by email and website notice. Continued use after effective date constitutes acknowledgment.


### 20. Privacy Contact
20.1 For access, correction, or deletion: privacy@shopsphere.in with registered mobile number and order ID where relevant. Response within 15 business days. Escalation: grievance@shopsphere.in.
