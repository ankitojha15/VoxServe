### 1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy defines when a return or cancellation is allowed and how it is completed.
1.2 Standard return window is 7 calendar days from delivery. Cancellation before dispatch is free.


### 2. Return Eligibility
2.1 **Eligible:**
- Unused, complete product in original packaging for change-of-mind within 7 calendar days.
- Defective, damaged, wrong, or missing-item cases with evidence, regardless of seal status.


2.2 **Not eligible:**
- Requests after 7 calendar days.
- Non-returnable categories for change-of-mind.
- Products damaged by customer misuse or missing serial numbers.


### 3. Return Window
3.1 7 calendar days from delivery date shown in tracking, inclusive of delivery day.
3.2 Return request must be created in account or with support within this window. Pickup may occur after window if request was timely.


### 4. Product Condition Requirements
4.1 Change-of-mind: unused, unwashed, undamaged, with all tags, manuals, freebies, and accessories.
4.2 Electronics and appliances for change-of-mind: factory seals intact, device not activated, no data loaded, battery not charged beyond initial test.
4.3 Defective or damaged claims: condition as received must be preserved; do not attempt self-repair.


### 5. Packaging Requirements
5.1 Original box, inner packaging, and accessories must be returned. Outer shipping box may be reused for return transit.
5.2 If original packaging is missing for change-of-mind, return may be rejected or subject to 10% deduction if resalable.


### 6. Proof of Purchase
6.1 Order ID and delivery confirmation are sufficient. Invoice is available in account.
6.2 Serial number must match dispatch record for electronics above ₹5,000.


### 7. Defective Products
7.1 Defect means manufacturing fault under normal use within 7 days, such as no power-on, display failure, or motor failure.
7.2 Requires photographs or video and description. Technical verification may be done by video call or service visit.
7.3 Resolution: replacement if stock available, otherwise full refund.


### 8. Damaged Products
8.1 Damage means transit breakage, dent, crack, or shattered screen on arrival.
8.2 Must be reported within 48 hours with outer-box and product photographs or unboxing video.
8.3 Resolution: free pickup, replacement or refund including shipping fee.


### 9. Wrong Products
9.1 Wrong means different model, colour, or size from order confirmation.
9.2 Requires photograph of received item and package label. Free pickup and replacement or refund.


### 10. Missing Products and Accessories
10.1 Includes empty box, missing item in multi-item order, or missing charger, remote, or manual.
10.2 Must be reported within 48 hours with photographs of package and contents.
10.3 Verified missing cases receive the missing part, replacement, or proportional refund.


### 11. Non-Returnable Products
11.1 For hygiene, safety, and consumable reasons, these are non-returnable for change-of-mind:
- Personal care, cosmetics if seal broken, innerwear, food, consumables, SIM cards, software licenses after activation, and products marked non-returnable on product page.
11.2 Non-returnable products remain eligible for defective, damaged, or wrong-item claims with evidence.


### 12. Electronics and Appliances
12.1 Can a customer return an opened electronic product? **Conditional:** Yes only if defective, damaged, or wrong. No for change-of-mind if seal is broken, device is activated, or installation is completed.
12.2 Large appliances requiring installation: return eligible only if installation is done by authorized personnel and defect is confirmed within window.
12.3 After 7 days, covered defects are handled under manufacturer warranty, not ShopSphere return.


### 13. Cancellation Before Dispatch
13.1 **Eligible:** customer may cancel free of charge before dispatch notification through account or support.
13.2 Prepaid amount plus shipping fee is refunded within 5-7 business days after approval.


### 14. Cancellation After Dispatch
14.1 Can a customer cancel after shipping? **No direct cancellation.** Order must be received and then returned under 7-day return process.
14.2 Refusal at door is recorded as failed delivery and follows Shipping Policy undeliverable handling.


### 15. Cancellation After Delivery
15.1 Treated as return. Must meet 7-day window, condition, and category rules.
15.2 COD orders after delivery follow the same return inspection; approved refunds go by bank transfer.


### 16. Return Shipping - Who Pays
16.1 ShopSphere pays for defective, damaged, wrong, or missing-item pickups.
16.2 Customer pays for change-of-mind: ₹79 deducted from refund or self-ship to designated warehouse with tracking.
16.3 Self-shipped returns must be delivered within 5 calendar days of approval; lost self-shipments are customer responsibility.


### 17. Quality Inspection
17.1 Inspection within 2 business days of warehouse receipt checks condition, completeness, serial match, and activation status.
17.2 Inspection result is shared by email with photographs in case of rejection.


### 18. Rejection Conditions
18.1 Return is rejected for: used or soiled product for change-of-mind, broken seals where required, missing accessories, late request, or serial mismatch. Customer may request re-shipment at own cost within 7 calendar days.


### 19. Replacement vs Refund
19.1 Replacement is offered only for defective, damaged, or wrong items if identical stock is available. Only one replacement per order.
19.2 Refund is issued if stock is unavailable, replacement fails inspection, or customer prefers refund. Change-of-mind returns receive refund only, no replacement.


### 20. Refund Timeline
20.1 Refund initiated within 2 business days of inspection pass. Credit in 5-7 business days to source account, or to verified bank account for COD.
20.2 Deductions for customer-paid return shipping are shown before initiation.


### 21. Cancellation Exceptions
21.1 Express-shipped orders cannot be cancelled after dispatch. Oversized appliance orders with scheduled installation cannot be cancelled within 24 hours of appointment.


### 22. Escalation Process
22.1 Step 1: support with order ID and return ID. Step 2: if unresolved in 3 business days, escalate to returns@shopsphere.in. Resolution within 5 business days with inspection records.
