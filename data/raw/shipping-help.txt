VoxServe Shipping Help - Official Guide

[Page 1]
1. Delivery Timelines:
- Metro cities: delivery within 2 business days.
- Non-metro cities: delivery within 3-5 business days.
- Remote pincodes may take up to 7 business days.

[Page 2]
2. Order Tracking:
- Live tracking ID is available via order-mcp tool using get_order_status.
- Customer needs order ID to fetch tracking status.
- If tracking shows stuck for more than 48 hours, create a support ticket.

[Page 3]
3. Failed Delivery:
- If delivery fails twice, order is returned to warehouse.
- Customer can request redelivery once via support.
- After 2 failed voice responses, auto-create ticket for human follow-up.
