VoxServe Refund Policy - Official Document

[Page 1]
1. Eligibility:
- Refund is applicable within 7 days of delivery if the product is damaged, defective, or wrong item delivered.
- Customer must provide order ID and unboxing photo for damage claims.
- Orders above #1000 are eligible for priority email support.

[Page 2]
2. Refund Timeline:
- UPI and card refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
- COD orders are refunded via bank transfer within 7 business days.
- Customer will receive tracking updates on email and SMS.

[Page 3]
3. Non-Refundable Cases:
- No refund for used products, missing tags, or after 7 days.
- No cash refund for coupon-discounted orders, only store credit.
- If confidence is low or policy is unclear, escalate to human agent. Do not assume refund approval.
