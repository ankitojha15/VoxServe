### 1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy states when ShopSphere issues a refund, how it is paid, and how long it takes.
1.2 A refund is initiated only after cancellation approval or successful return inspection.
1.3 Business days means Monday to Saturday, excluding national and Karnataka public holidays.


### 2. Refund Eligibility
2.1 **Eligible:**
- Prepaid order cancelled before dispatch and approved.
- Returned product that passes quality inspection within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- Defective, damaged, wrong-item, or missing-item case approved after evidence verification.
- Order declared lost under the Shipping Policy.
- Proven duplicate payment or excess charge.


2.2 **Not eligible:**
- Return requested after 7 calendar days from delivery.
- Non-returnable category products for change-of-mind reasons.
- Products failing inspection due to customer use, damage, missing accessories, or removed serial numbers.
- Requests without a valid ShopSphere order ID.


2.3 **Requires verification:**
- All damaged, defective, and wrong-item claims require photographs or video within 48 hours of delivery.
- Orders above ₹20,000 may require serial-number and invoice verification.


### 3. Refund Methods - Prepaid Orders
3.1 UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking, and wallet payments are refunded to the same source instrument.
3.2 If the source account is closed, customer must provide an alternate bank account in the same name within 7 calendar days. Refund is made by bank transfer after verification.
3.3 EMI orders: refund is credited to the linked card or loan account. Bank EMI interest or foreclosure charges are not refunded by ShopSphere.


### 4. Refund Methods - Cash on Delivery (COD)
4.1 COD refunds are not paid in cash.
4.2 COD refunds are processed by bank transfer or approved digital method to an account in the order recipient's name.
4.3 Customer must submit bank account number, IFSC, and UPI ID within 7 calendar days of approval. Failure to provide details pauses the refund until details are received.
4.4 Name mismatch requires additional ID proof and adds 2 business days for verification.


### 5. Refund Processing Timeline
5.1 Inspection completion: within 2 business days of return pickup receipt at warehouse.
5.2 Refund initiation: within 2 business days of cancellation approval or inspection pass.
5.3 Credit to customer: 5-7 business days after refund initiation.
5.4 Total typical time from return delivery to credit: 7-9 business days.


### 6. Bank, Card and Wallet Timelines
6.1 After ShopSphere initiates the refund:
- UPI and wallets: 3-5 business days.
- Debit card, credit card, net banking: 5-7 business days.
- COD bank transfer: 5-7 business days after details verification.
6.2 Delays beyond 7 business days must be checked with the issuing bank first using the ShopSphere refund reference ID.


### 7. Partial Refunds
7.1 Partial refund applies when:
- Only one item of a multi-item order is returned.
- Product is returned with minor packaging damage but is resalable, subject to up to 10% deduction with prior notice.
- Promotional bundle is partially returned; bundle discount is adjusted proportionally.
7.2 Any deduction is communicated by email with reason before initiation.


### 8. Damaged, Defective, Wrong or Missing Items
8.1 Customer must report within 48 hours of delivery with order ID, photographs or unboxing video, and description.
8.2 **Eligible:** manufacturing defect, transit damage, wrong model, or sealed-box missing accessories.
8.3 **Resolution:** replacement if stock is available, otherwise full refund including original shipping fee.
8.4 Return pickup for these cases is scheduled by ShopSphere at no cost to the customer.


### 9. Failed Delivery Refunds
9.1 If 3 delivery attempts fail or the package is undeliverable due to incorrect address and is returned to warehouse, prepaid order value is refunded.
9.2 Original shipping fee of ₹79, if paid, is non-refundable for customer-caused failed delivery.
9.3 COD failed-delivery orders carry no refund because no advance was collected.


### 10. Cancelled-Order Refunds
10.1 Cancellation before dispatch: full prepaid amount plus any shipping fee is refunded. Initiation within 2 business days of approval.
10.2 Cancellation after dispatch cannot be cancelled directly; customer must follow return processing after delivery.
10.3 Cancellation after delivery is treated as a return and requires 7-day window compliance.


### 11. Duplicate Payments
11.1 If two charges are confirmed for one order ID, the duplicate is refunded in full within 5-7 business days.
11.2 Customer must provide both transaction IDs and payment screenshots.


### 12. Refund Exceptions
12.1 No refund for:
- Non-returnable products listed in Return & Cancellation Policy Section 10.
- Change-of-mind returns for opened electronics that require intact seals.
- Gift-wrap charges and COD handling fees, if any.
- Delay caused by incorrect bank details provided by customer.


### 13. Shipping Charges and Return Shipping Charges
13.1 Original shipping: orders above ₹999 have free shipping. Orders at or below ₹999 carry ₹79 fee.
13.2 If a prepaid order above ₹999 is fully cancelled before dispatch, no shipping is charged.
13.3 Return shipping for defective, damaged, or wrong items is paid by ShopSphere.
13.4 Return shipping for change-of-mind is paid by customer: ₹79 deducted from refund or pickup arranged at customer cost.


### 14. Refund Status and Escalation
14.1 Status is available in account order history and by email with refund reference ID.
14.2 If refund is not credited within 7 business days of initiation: Step 1 - contact support with reference ID. Step 2 - if unresolved in 3 business days, escalate to refunds@shopsphere.in with bank statement. Step 3 - resolution within 5 business days.


### 15. Required Customer Information
15.1 Order ID, registered mobile number or email, reason, and photographs where applicable.
15.2 For COD: bank account holder name, account number, IFSC, and UPI ID.
15.3 ShopSphere never asks for card CVV, OTP, or net-banking password for refunds.
