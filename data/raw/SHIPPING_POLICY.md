### 1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy defines where ShopSphere delivers, how long it takes, charges, and handling of exceptions.
1.2 All timelines use business days unless stated as calendar days.


### 2. Delivery Regions
2.1 ShopSphere delivers to all serviceable PIN codes in India.
2.2 Serviceability is confirmed at checkout. Non-serviceable PIN codes cannot place orders.
2.3 No international shipping.


### 3. Order Processing Time
3.1 Order processing is 1-2 business days after payment confirmation for prepaid orders and after order confirmation for COD.
3.2 Processing includes verification, packing, and handover to carrier. Dispatch notification is sent by SMS and email.
3.3 Cancellation before dispatch is allowed during this stage.


### 4. Standard Delivery Timeline
4.1 Standard domestic shipping is 3-7 business days after dispatch.
- Metro cities: 3-4 business days.
- Non-metro cities: 4-6 business days.
- Remote areas: 6-7 business days.
4.2 Total time from order placement is processing plus shipping, typically 4-9 business days.


### 5. Express Delivery
5.1 Express delivery is available only for selected PIN codes and products marked Express at checkout.
5.2 Express timeline: 2-3 business days after dispatch. Additional fee of ₹149 applies even for orders above ₹999.
5.3 Express is not available for remote-area PIN codes or oversized appliances.


### 6. Shipping Charges and Free-Shipping Threshold
6.1 Orders above ₹999: free standard shipping.
6.2 Orders at or below ₹999: ₹79 standard shipping fee.
6.3 Threshold is calculated on order value after discounts, excluding taxes and fees.
6.4 Split shipments due to ShopSphere logistics do not incur extra charges.


### 7. Tracking
7.1 Tracking ID is provided within 24 hours of dispatch by SMS, email, and account order history.
7.2 Tracking updates every 24-48 hours. If no update for 72 hours, customer may contact support.
7.3 Tracking status of stuck for more than 48 hours qualifies for support-ticket creation.


### 8. Delivery Attempts
8.1 Carrier makes 3 delivery attempts on 3 consecutive business days.
8.2 Customer receives SMS before each attempt. Customer must be available or authorize a recipient with ID.
8.3 After 3 failed attempts, package is returned to warehouse and treated as undeliverable.


### 9. Address Changes
9.1 **Eligible:** address change is allowed only before dispatch through account support.
9.2 **Not eligible:** address change after dispatch. Customer must wait for delivery attempt or return and reorder.
9.3 PIN code change after dispatch is not permitted.


### 10. Delayed Shipments
10.1 A shipment is delayed if it exceeds 7 business days after dispatch without delivery.
10.2 Customer may raise a ticket after 7 business days. ShopSphere traces with carrier within 2 business days.
10.3 If delayed beyond 10 business days, customer may choose to wait, receive replacement if available, or receive full prepaid refund.


### 11. Lost Shipments
11.1 A shipment is declared lost if untraceable for 10 business days after dispatch.
11.2 Prepaid orders receive full refund including shipping fee. COD orders are cancelled without charge.
11.3 Replacement is offered if identical stock is available.


### 12. Damaged Packages
12.1 If outer package is visibly damaged at delivery, customer must note it with carrier, photograph the package, and accept only after inspection where possible.
12.2 Report within 48 hours with photographs. ShopSphere arranges free return pickup and replacement or refund.


### 13. Incorrect Address
13.1 Customer is responsible for correct address, PIN code, and phone number at checkout.
13.2 If address is incorrect and package returns, redelivery is arranged once after address correction. Redelivery fee of ₹79 applies.


### 14. Undeliverable Packages
14.1 Packages undeliverable due to wrong address, recipient refusal, or 3 failed attempts are returned to warehouse.
14.2 Prepaid value is refunded minus ₹79 return handling where failure was customer-caused. COD orders are closed.


### 15. Remote-Area Restrictions
15.1 Remote PIN codes may take up to 7 business days and support only standard shipping.
15.2 Oversized home appliances above 30 kg require scheduled delivery by appointment.
15.3 COD may be unavailable for selected remote PIN codes.


### 16. Force-Majeure Delays
16.1 Delays due to floods, strikes, government restrictions, or transport disruption suspend standard timelines.
16.2 ShopSphere notifies affected customers and resumes dispatch on clearance. Cancellation before dispatch remains available.


### 17. Escalation
17.1 Step 1: contact support with order ID and tracking ID. Step 2: if unresolved in 2 business days, escalate to shipping@shopsphere.in. Resolution within 3 business days.
